# Math 9 Review

A generated practice question bank for **Mathematics 9** (Newfoundland and Labrador), built on the same chassis as the Math 1201, 2200, 2201, 3200, 3201, and 3208 Review apps, with a student-facing front page and extra scaffolding for younger students.

**Live app:** https://cdli-nlschools.github.io/Math9Review/ (once GitHub Pages is enabled)

## What it does

- Opens on a simple student face: the selected scope, one line about what will happen, and one large start button. The full builder sits collapsed under "Build your own practice".
- Draws fresh practice questions by unit or by curriculum indicator, with a level mix following the published 2026-2027 tables of specifications (about 20% Level 1, 60% Level 2, 20% Level 3).
- Mid-course preset: Units 1 to 4 (Rational Numbers; Square Roots and Surface Area; Powers and Exponent Laws; Polynomials). Final preset: Units 5 to 9 (bank arrives with the final-scope build; the preset currently explains itself rather than erroring).
- Three ways to work: practice online with check-as-you-go marking, print a practice sheet (with working space and an optional answer key), or print a practice exam built to the tables of specifications (18 selected-response marks and 17 constructed-response marks, 35 in total, with a separate teacher key). Unit tests print at 25 marks. Sheets and exams can also be downloaded as a Word file with native editable equations, or copied for Google Docs.
- Every practice question carries a staged hint (strategy, then first step), a "See a worked example" button that shows the same skill with different numbers, per-answer feedback on multiple choice questions, and a full worked solution. The worked example and the worked solution are show/hide toggles: hiding and re-showing never changes what was dealt, and a separate "Show another example" button inside the example panel is the only way to deal a fresh example (in seeded sets the sequence of examples is reproducible, press by press). The end-of-set summary lists any indicators missed and offers a "Practice what I missed" set. A collapsible "Formulas and facts for this unit" panel sits at the top of each practice set.
- Practice exams offer an optional soft timer that counts up and notes the 1.5-hour exam length. It never locks or submits anything.
- Teachers can set a seed word or phrase in the builder ("Same problems for everyone"): a shared link carrying that seed gives every student the identical set, same questions, same values, same order, including the worked examples. With no seed, each student gets a fresh random set.
- Any practice set or practice exam can be downloaded for Kahoot (.xlsx, in Kahoot's template layout) or Blooket (.csv, in Blooket's template layout) to run the same multiple choice questions as a game, with the math written as readable plain text and a time limit set by question level (20, 30 or 60 seconds). Constructed-response questions are not exported, and the app says how many were left out.

## Sources and originality

Items are generated from templates written for this bank, informed by the Mathematics 9 curriculum outcomes and indicators (NL eNLightenED viewer and the course units/indicators spreadsheet) and the 2026-2027 Mathematics 7-12 tables of specifications. Question types are also informed by a type-level harvest of the 2011 to 2014 Math 9 mid-course papers, the NLSchools Math 9 intervention self assessments and unit reviews, and the Grade 9 development shell's quiz organization on Brightspace. No question text, scenario, or values from any source are reproduced.
